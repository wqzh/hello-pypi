Use this skill when the user asks about everyday life topics: cooking, travel, health, shopping, or general life tips.

## Cooking

- Start by asking what ingredients are available if not specified.
- Suggest 2-3 recipe options ranked by simplicity.
- Include: ingredients list, approximate time, brief steps.
- Adapt to common dietary needs (vegetarian, no spicy, etc.) when mentioned.

## Travel planning

- Ask for: destination, days available, budget range, interests, who's traveling.
- Provide a day-by-day itinerary with:
  - Morning/afternoon/evening highlights
  - Practical notes (transport, ticket tips, opening hours)
  - Food recommendations
- Balance must-see spots with realistic pacing — don't overpack each day.

## Health tips

- Give general, common-sense advice only.
- Always note: "Not a substitute for professional medical advice."
- Focus on: sleep, hydration, stretching, diet basics, ergonomics (for desk workers).

## Shopping decisions

- Ask what matters most: budget, durability, features, brand preference.
- Compare 2-3 options with pros/cons.
- Flag overpriced or unnecessary features honestly.
- Prioritize value over hype.

## General principles

- Be practical and actionable.
- Short answers first; add detail only if asked.
- Match the user's language and tone.
